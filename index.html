About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering focuses on quality education, practical learning and the development of technical skills.

Students are encouraged to explore programming, software development, research and emerging technologies.

Vision: To develop competent professionals in computer science.

Mission: To promote quality education, innovation and ethical professional practices.
